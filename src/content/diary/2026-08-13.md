Today I spent most of my time building out the new Document Management System. I shipped the core documents app with company-scoped S3 storage, immutable versioning, rollback, soft delete, checksum-based deduplication, and a 25 MB/MIME-based upload policy. I also kept the app loosely coupled from the rest of the project, so it can be reused in another project by copying the app and providing the storage setting, without needing changes to the rest of the system.

After that, I connected the library with ISO manuals and evidence. Manuals can now reference library documents directly, while evidence was migrated from Cloudinary URLs to proper document relationships without losing existing data. I updated the downstream consumers around scoring, reporting, RAG, cross-framework propagation, and audit logs, including a couple of paths that were silently failing before.

I also cleaned up some existing issues around migrations and Django admin, removed three N+1 query problems, and added regression tests for the list endpoints. Ended the day with 248 tests passing, including roughly 50 new tests.
