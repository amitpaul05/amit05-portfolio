Client feedback on the Statement of Applicability exports on Govara came down to one question: are a PDF and an Excel one document or two. One. A single version record produces both now, with the spreadsheet hanging off it as a generic companion_file, so the documents app never learns what a Statement of Applicability is. Merged to dev as 261. RISK_ASSESSMENT and RISK_TREATMENT became one type too, converted in place since both old shapes are subsets of the merged one.

The rest went to courier delivery on Idlewild, mostly decisions. Booking at checkout is the usual pattern and it is wrong here, since nothing is packed yet, so a new ready_for_pickup state does the booking and the courier's own events drive the rest. No building against a live integration either: a mock backend and an event simulator instead. The backend is migrated and checked against the running container, which turned up two real bugs. Also killed an old N+1, 102 queries down to 12.
