Client asked to drop the Recommendations tab from the compliance audit report. There is no such tab in an actual report, so that went back to them. The SoA one was real. The single Justification field became four Yes/No driver columns, business requirements, risk assessment, legal and contractual, plus a reason field shown only when a control is not applicable. Applicable still comes from the self-assessment answer: drivers record why a control applies, they do not decide it.

The real bug: a document owner could open Assign Reviewer, pick themselves and approve from Draft, skipping review entirely, admins included. Only the uploader was blocked. I added a revision reason for policies yesterday, so I reverted that and put it in the shared lifecycle engine, where documents get it too.

Two accounts sharing a display name were impossible to tell apart as Owner or Reviewer, so the email is appended. That brought an N+1 into get_full_name, down to one query per company per request. On the GDPR complaint, IP and the other personal fields get hidden in the serializers rather than shutting a plain user out of the log. Still open: risk registers opening with a starter risk list, and the log events we never captured, reviewer self-assignment among them.
