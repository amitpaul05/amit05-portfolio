Today the multidomain work went from plan to live on Shikago. A partner points their DNS at our server, adds the domain from the merchant dashboard, and the storefront comes up on their own name with its own certificate. Django resolves the partner from its own table rather than Lua inside nginx, and the frontend sends a slug instead of the host. A domain that matches nothing fails outright rather than redirecting home, so a visitor on a partner site never learns the other sites exist.

That box is shared with sites that have nothing to do with us, so every server step was checked before it ran. I curled all of them and kept the output, looked at what was listening and on which address, and armed a timed rollback with at before enabling ufw, in case I shut myself out. After the change I curled them again and diffed. Nothing moved.

Verification took most of the day. The TXT record we told the partner to add was landing one level too deep, because the DNS panel appends the zone to whatever host you type, so the record sat at a name nobody was asking for. It still failed after that: the checker was reading through the local resolver and getting a cached answer, so it asks the domain's own nameservers now. Verify could also be clicked while a check was already running, so it is disabled while one is in flight, and the card updates itself rather than telling the user to refresh in a minute.

For a real test I took shopperworld.co, removed its existing nginx block so it behaved like a domain arriving for the first time, turned ACME staging off and ran the whole flow. It came up. Then every image broke, because the domain proxy was catching next/image requests and the media host was being guessed. The proxy stays off image paths now and the host comes from the public API endpoint.

Finished by writing the runbook for whoever debugs this next, short enough that they will actually read it. Subdomains of sikago.app are the next one.
